# Unit Economics

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits at a **15% average margin**. Our goal is to convert "High Savings" teams identified by the audit into Credex platform users.

**LTV Calculation (Per Customer):**
- **Average Spend**: A typical Series A startup buys $800/mo in AI credits (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) through Credex.
- **Monthly Revenue**: $800 × 15% = **$120/mo**.
- **Average Retention**: 18 months (typical startup lifecycle at this stage).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: $120 × 18 = **$2,160**.

## CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) by Channel

| Channel | Est. CAC | Basis |
|---------|----------|-------|
| HN / Reddit organic | $0 | Purely sweat equity / time. |
| Twitter content flywheel | $0 | High-quality threads with data insights. |
| Cold DM outreach | $15 | ~2 hours of research/outreach for 10 DMs, 1 conversion. |
| Product Hunt | $30 | Pre-launch prep time + hunter relationship. |
| Paid Search (Future) | $180 | B2B SaaS industry benchmark for "cloud cost" keywords. |

**Blended CAC (Organic Mix): ~$12 per customer.** This gives us a massive **LTV:CAC ratio of ~180:1**, leaving plenty of room for paid scaling later.

## Conversion Funnel Targets

1. **Audit Completed → Email Captured**: 20%
2. **Email Captured → Consultation Booked**: 15%
3. **Consultation Booked → Credit Purchase (Lead to Customer)**: 40%

**Overall Conversion (Audit → Purchase): 1.2%**

**At 1,000 audits/month:**
- 200 emails captured.
- 30 consultations booked.
- 12 credit purchases.
- **Revenue**: 12 new customers × $120/mo × 18mo = **$25,920 LTV generated per month**.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

- **Month 1-3**: 500 audits/mo → 6 new customers/mo (Initial seeding + Show HN).
- **Month 4-6**: 2,000 audits/mo → 24 new customers/mo (Scaling through Reddit + WoM).
- **Month 7-12**: 5,000 audits/mo → 60 new customers/mo (Twitter flywheel + Referrals).
- **Month 13-18**: 10,000 audits/mo → 120 new customers/mo (SEO takes over + Product Hunt long tail).

**Cumulative customers by month 18: ~720**
**Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: 720 × $120 = **$86,400/mo**
**Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: **~$1,036,800 ✓**

## Critical Assumptions (What must be true)
1. **Completion Rate**: Audit completion stays above 60% (UX remains simple).
2. **Capture Rate**: Email capture stays above 15% (Audit summary provides real value).
3. **Consultation Utility**: Credex consultation → purchase rate stays above 35%.
4. **Churn**: Monthly churn stays below 8%.
5. **Content**: Our data-driven content (e.g., "State of Startup AI Spend") remains viral enough to drive 10k audits/mo.
